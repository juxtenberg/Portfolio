App Design, UI/UX

MyData Assistant

MyData Assistant lets teams request analytics in plain language. The idea was strong; the interface wasn't. I redesigned the core experience around clarity, control and a scalable design system.

ClientAnalytics Intelligence
RoleUX/UI Design
Year2020

Challenges

  • Basic tasks, like customising a channel or setting a KPI, took too many steps.
  • Powerful features existed but were hard to find.
  • Layered navigation and no shared design language made the product feel improvised.
  • Strong features had low usage.

Process

  • Audited pain points using existing research and a first-time-user walkthrough.
  • Brought engineering in early to confirm feasibility.
  • Mapped journeys and ranked problems by impact versus effort.
  • Prototyped and tested workflow variants with stakeholders.
  • Built a lightweight design system to unify the product.

What I redesigned

  • Customisable homepage — a configurable dashboard instead of a fixed one.
  • Role management — permission tables became an editable, drag-and-drop org chart.
  • Task manager — Pending, Active and Completed tabs instead of one date-sorted list.
  • AI chat — a conversational UI to generate, view and download reports.
  • Settings — grouped into user and organisation tabs, with search and descriptions.
  • Notifications — an app-style inbox with read/unread states and grouping.

Outcomes

  • Faster, more intuitive workflows with more control over views.
  • Higher engagement with previously underused features like custom KPI tracking and multi-channel reporting.
  • Fewer settings-related support tickets.
  • Better visual consistency and faster feature development thanks to the design system.
