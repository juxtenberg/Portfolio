Futina eStore

Futina Electricals, a manufacturer of electrical accessories, had a distributor-style website that produced no digital sales. I led its redesign into a mobile-first eCommerce store built for Nigerian shoppers — delivering a 98% uplift in flagship product sales within a month of launch.

ClientWPI x Futina
RoleProduct Designer
Year2024 · ~12 weeks

The problem

  • Weak product taxonomy — products were listed by SKU only.
  • Low-quality photography and no video in a category where trust matters.
  • No Futina branding, so the site read like an unofficial reseller.
  • No certifications, awards or reviews.
  • A desktop-first layout that broke on mobile, where most traffic came from.

Process

  • Competitive analysis of Nigerian electrical eCommerce players.
  • Personas and journey maps for two buyers: the Technician (trade) and the Homeowner (non-technical).
  • Card sorting and tree testing, leading to use-case categories and mega-menus.
  • Dual-literacy prototyping: specs available but secondary, with imagery and plain language leading.
  • Pre-launch validation with Attention Insight and layout A/B comparisons.
  • Continuous optimisation with Hotjar, Google Analytics and VWO, run as a recurring CRO routine.

The solution

  • Mobile-first rebuild with bottom navigation, slide-in menu, quick view and a guest wishlist.
  • Use-case taxonomy with progressive filtering and image-based variant selection.
  • Product video and 360° views on every listing.
  • Integration with Futina's inventory and CRM, showing live stock status.
  • Certifications, awards and verified reviews on the homepage and product pages.

Results

  • 98% uplift in flagship product sales one month after launch.
  • Significant increase in customer engagement and retention.
  • Positive shift in sentiment, confirmed by post-launch surveys.
