Moni by [AI]

MONI is a rewards platform from Analytics Intelligence's [AI] Collaborative Customer Intelligence initiative, offering personalised advertising and sales campaigns.

ClientLandmark Group · Landmark Africa (pilot)
RoleUX/UI Designer
Year2020
LiveView site ↗

Challenge & approach

The challenge was aligning Landmark's diverse audience needs with MONI's capabilities, and using real-time data to personalise rewards. Competitor analysis, user research and iterative prototyping led to a streamlined, intuitive design built to boost engagement.

Outcome

A user-centred app for Landmark Africa with seamless reward redemption and personalised offers — laying the groundwork for MONI's adoption across more sectors.
