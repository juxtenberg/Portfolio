Azany Seller Central

Azany is a global eCommerce platform for buying country-specific products from around the world. Its seller platform gives vendors one place to manage products, sales and orders. I joined mid-project as lead product designer to turn an outdated platform into a usable, scalable marketplace.

ClientAzany
RoleUI/UX
Year2023

Challenges

  • Weak shopping experience and a homepage that didn't engage.
  • An outdated, inconsistent design system that was hard to build with.
  • Incomplete seller dashboard prototypes.
  • A tight timeline, with development already underway.

Approach

  • Audited the marketplace and seller dashboard flows and user feedback.
  • Ran workshops with designers and developers to balance usability, business goals and feasibility.
  • Updated the design system with reusable, scalable components.
  • Rebuilt Seller Central: stats homepage, product management, orders, customers and settings.
  • Delivered working prototypes for fast feedback and developer handoff.

Outcome

  • Revitalised homepage and shopping flows to drive engagement and cross-country sales.
  • More intuitive storefront tools for sellers, supporting satisfaction and retention.
  • A refreshed design system that sped up development and kept the platform consistent.
  • Audit, redesign and handoff delivered quickly in support of launch goals.
